Energy

PV → critical and flexible loads → battery → export; grid import within the transformer and voltage limits. No reverse flow beyond the sanctioned export cap.

Data

Meters and plugs → gateway → forecast + MILP → asset commands. DISCOM sends stress windows and receives risk, loading, loss and DR analytics.

Money

Household tiered fee (lifeline ₹0) + bill saving + avoided outage cost + DISCOM peak/loss payment → asset capex, O&M and the local technician.
